How to play

  1. Memorise the three-number combination.
  2. Wait for the dial to start moving.
  3. Press STOP when the dial reaches the first number.
  4. Repeat for the second and third numbers.
  5. Unlock all three positions to open the vault.

You must stop the dial in the correct order. Each number has a small tolerance, depending on difficulty.
